The first time they were closed. The second time they were taking the day off. Finally, the third time I got [生煎包] from the Michelin-recognized? [鍾原上海生煎包?]. The first batch lukewarm I ate right there. The second batch piping hot I ate at the nearby park, using the bag to hold the precious buns.

A fresh SJB? is a transient beauty. Like a fresh New York pizza slice, you should eat it quickly before the wet and dry ingredients merge into a sog.[^1]
[^1]: from soggy

I'd say my mom’s SJB? are better on a good day, when she puts in 香菇 and manages to not break them while serving, and I manage to procure the right hot sauce. My mom would cook SJB? for me whenever I visited or took an important interview from my parents' home. These SJB? serve as a great meal before my important interview tonight.
