Before going to the beach in Busan, I told my friend I'm a "medium beach guy", so I'd probably stay on the sand instead of going into the water.

Even with the beach umbrella and a generous layer of sunscreen, I felt like I was baking. So it didn't take much convincing from my friend to enter the water. Perhaps I'd enjoy it; perhaps I'd discover myself a regular "beach guy".

Well, I entered the water and felt like I was baking and wet at the same time. So I'm just "not a beach guy".

One big point of traveling is to discover. However, there's more to discover than I have time for. Knowing myself, such as knowing I'm "not a beach guy",[^1] gives me the heuristics to discover stuff more interesting, more meaningful to me.
[^1]: also "not a club guy" and a "medium concert guy"
