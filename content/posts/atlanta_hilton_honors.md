As I've toured, I've noticed increasing consolidation under large hotel companies. [These franchising and management, rather than ownership, agreements give owners better operations, including better integration with online booking and travel agencies. Meanwhile, the large hotel companies expand the network of their loyalty programs, one of their biggest drivers of growth.](https://finance.yahoo.com/real-estate/articles/why-global-hotel-chains-keep-081208317.html)

Of course each of these large hotel companies have hotels across tiers. However, I find Hyatt and Marriott tend toward luxury, Hilton toward midscale, and IHG and Wyndham toward economy. As an [enjoyer of Threshold]({{< ref "threshold_of_my_bedroom.md" >}}), I find myself in the midscale to upper-midscale - below luxury you're not paying for fluff, and above economy you're not dealing with dysfunctional parts or people who don't care. Luxury consumers may counter that their fluff has value, and economy consumers may counter that midscale still has fluff. Of course other people are wrong, objectively wrong.

So I signed up for Hilton's free rewards program, Hilton Honors, after staying at Canopy by Hilton Atlanta Midtown. That should give me some extra value, though I should avoid blind loyalty to Hilton. I booked better-value independent hotels in Savannah and Charleston.
