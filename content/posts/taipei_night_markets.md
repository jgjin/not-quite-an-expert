You can find some of the best food in Taipei in night markets. [For example, Fuzhou Pepper Buns has received the Michelin Bib Gourmand.](https://www.theworldofstreetfood.com/2026/01/taiwanese-black-pepper-pork-buns.html)

These delicious buns show very good food need not be expensive or fancy, rising from competition and [hyperspecialization]({{< ref "hyperspecialization.md" >}}). However, watching the stall's workers run an assembly line,[^1] I see that if food is a labor of love, it is indeed _labor_. I feel grateful that I do not know such physical labor.
[^1]: including sweating over clay ovens in the humid summer heat!
