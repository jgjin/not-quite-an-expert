They're in [the Representative List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity](https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/culture-of-jeju-haenyeo-women-divers-01068) and, more importantly, they're in [a Japanese Breakfast song](https://genius.com/Japanese-breakfast-diving-woman-lyrics). The haenyeo, the diving women, of Jeju dive without oxygen masks to harvest seafood for their livelihood. Their economic importance has led to the semi-matriarchal structure of Jeju.

That semi-matriarchal structure stands in strong contrast to most of Korean society, which has been patriarchal since the Neo-Confucian Joseon Dynasty. In fact, [despite notable exceptions like the matrilineal belt in Africa, many more societies are patriarchal than matriarchal](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=6461799). Many theories try to explain why; [one theory proposes pre-industrial societies evolved toward more people, more labor, and more military, pushing women toward domestic roles and men toward physical labor and warrior roles](https://www.bbc.com/future/article/20230525-how-did-patriarchy-actually-begin). Those roles echo even today.

[Some feminists point out modern technology has relieved the pressure for more people, more labor, and more military, rendering patriarchal structures less useful, perhaps obsolete.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Dialectic_of_Sex) [However, contemporary feminists emphasize that patriarchal structures adapt to keep men in power, such as in unrealistic beauty standards targeting women.](https://www.jstor.org/stable/42853921)

The haenyeo are in decline. [As time marches on, their numbers dwindle and their average age goes up.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haenyeo#Declining_numbers) However, in that decline is progress. [The girls are getting better educations and finding less demanding, more financially rewarding jobs than diving.](https://doi.org/10.1353%2Fks.2006.0006)[^1] One day we may hear the last sumbisori off the coast of Jeju. Let's hope that day the women are doing better.
[^1]: [though not always](https://www.theguardian.com/environment/2025/may/15/south-koreas-female-freedivers-tv-has-made-stars-of-the-haenyeo-but-what-is-their-real-story)
