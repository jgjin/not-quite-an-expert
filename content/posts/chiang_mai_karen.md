As part of my Chiang Rai tour from Chiang Mai, we visited a "long neck village" of Karen women. Some Karen, or Kayan (the village used both terms), women wear permanent neck rings which lengthen their necks by compressing their rib cages.

I share [the concerns of this blogger](https://powerstotravel.com/asia/southeastasia/thailand/ethics-visit-kayan-long-neck-women-near-chiang-mai) - the neck rings undermine the comfort and health of the women. However, they also attract tourists and their money, [providing income for the refugees](https://asiatimes.com/2018/01/forget-boycott-come-visit-us-long-neck-kayan-says).

Ultimately, I gave them a bit of my money - they seemed to need it. However, I avoided feeding the spectacle - instead of taking pictures of the women, I took a picture of [the photo stand-in](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Photo_stand-in) for tourists to look like the long neck women.
