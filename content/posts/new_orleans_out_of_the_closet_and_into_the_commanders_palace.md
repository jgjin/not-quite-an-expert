I am finally, finally traveling, and I have chosen New Orleans as my first destination because the last time I traveled the US I avoided the South,[^1] and New Orleans has a unique food culture.
[^1]: COVID-19 was still a major concern when I last traveled the US.

I was literally planning my trip to New Orleans on the plane, chatting with Google Gemini over spotty in-flight Wi-Fi to figure out what to do and where to eat. In repeated sessions, Gemini consistently mentioned the Commander's Palace, which also appears on [the Eater list for New Orleans](https://nola.eater.com/maps/best-restaurants-new-orleans-38-map-nola).

I snagged a reservation for myself at 9:15 PM. However, my additional research warned me the Commander's Palace has a [dress code](https://www.commanderspalace.com/about), and will turn away people violating that code. The software engineer I am, I only pack t-shirts, so I had to procure a collared shirt.

[Further research](https://www.reddit.com/r/AskNOLA/comments/1pvzbvy/snagged_a_reservation_at_commanders_palace_last) suggested I go to Out of the Closet, which I felt good about because I would be [reusing]({{< ref "five_rs.md" >}}) clothes and contributing ever so slightly to their mission. So I ventured there by bus, and when I got to their door…

You must remember they sometimes don't get any traffic the half hour before close. You must also remember the staff deserve a break every once in a while, perhaps if they don't get any traffic the half hour before close. You must especially try to remember this as you're standing in front of their door under the humid heat of New Orleans summer, failing to find the staff to unlock their door the half hour before close. You must really, really try.

So I headed to another thrift store nearby. This one had the choice of either a garish red dress shirt in my size, or a subtle cream dress shirt one size too large. I chose the second, hoping to emulate [the fashion of an oversized Oxford shirt]({{< ref "uniqlo_x_jw_anderson.md" >}}). Only after buying it did I realize it was too oversized, almost like a blouse.

I entered the Commander's Palace in my thrifted dress shirt/blouse. Perhaps they admired me; I find if you wear something unusual with enough confidence people assume it's fashionable. Perhaps they pitied me; "bless your heart!" Most importantly, they let me in, and I had lacquered quail with turtle soup, bread pudding soufflé, an awkward handshake after initially failing to notice the waiter was extending his hand to shake, and a bill correction that the lacquered quail included the turtle soup.
