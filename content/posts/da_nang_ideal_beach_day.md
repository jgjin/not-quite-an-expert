Even though [I'm a medium beach guy]({{< ref "busan_medium_beach_guy.md" >}}), I split a nice hotel room on My Khe Beach with my friend, because Da Nang is a beach city like Busan.

While my friend was out doing their own thing, I found my ideal beach day: sitting in my air-conditioned hotel room, overlooking the beach from a high floor through floor-to-ceiling windows, sipping my own AeroPress-brewed coffee, listening to my own music.

I didn't need to negotiate with the beach umbrellas for shade, or with the other beachgoers for room, or with the limited-menu vendors for drinks, or with the boomboxes for peace. For a little while, the space was entirely my own.
