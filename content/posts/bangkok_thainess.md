In between temples, I visited Museum Siam for their main exhibition ["Decoding Thainess"](https://museumsiam.org/exhibition-detail.php?CONID=2545&CID=203).

Those who have taken too many philosophy courses[^1] would recognize the introduction video like a Socratic dialogue - on one side a woman taking the traditional view of Thainess, on the other side a man taking the modern view of Thainess. Is Thainess [the royal barges](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Barge_Procession) or [the long-tail boats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Long-tail_boat)?
[^1]: those who have taken a philosophy course

Thainess escapes easy definition. For example, if we define Thainess around people born in Thailand, what about [Silpa Bhirasri, the father of modern art in Thailand, born Corrado Feroci in Italy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Silpa_Bhirasri)? If we define Thainess around people living in Thailand, what about Thai Americans?

Like any other national identity, Thainess is neither well-defined nor without definition. We could, and I think we should, relax the definition from binary (1 or 0, yes or no) to scored (e.g. percentage from 100 to 0).
